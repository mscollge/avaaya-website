Author Guidelines

Instructions for manuscript preparation

Manuscript Preparation

  • Manuscript must be original and unpublished.
  • Paper should include title, author details, abstract, keywords, introduction, methodology, results, conclusion and references.
  • Figures and tables must be clear and properly numbered.
  • References should follow a standard academic format.
  • Plagiarism and publication ethics policy must be followed.

File Format

Authors may submit manuscript in MS Word or PDF format through email or online submission form.
